Midiful Support

An offline iPhone app that turns MIDI files into piano practice.

Something not working, or a feature you wish existed? Open an issue and it goes straight to the developer.

Report a problem or ask a question

Opening an issue needs a free GitHub account. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you are on, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

Do I need a MIDI keyboard?

No, but the app is built around one. Midiful plays the accompaniment and shows you the piano part — it deliberately does not play the piano for you. Without a keyboard you can still read along, loop sections and watch the notes; with one, the app follows what you actually play.

How do I connect my keyboard?

Connect it over USB, using Apple's Lightning or USB-C camera adapter if your keyboard has a full-size USB-B socket. Midiful finds it automatically and the status shows in the player. Bluetooth MIDI is not supported yet.

My keyboard is connected but nothing happens

  • Some keyboards need external power when connected through an adapter — a USB hub that supplies power usually fixes it.
  • Check the keyboard is in MIDI mode rather than a mode that only sends audio.
  • iOS may cut power to USB devices while the app is in the background. Bring Midiful back to the foreground and reconnect.

How do I get songs in?

Share or open any .mid or .midi file with Midiful from Files, Mail or AirDrop, or use Import in the library. Three public-domain demo songs are included so you can start straight away. Multi-song MIDI files (SMF type 2) are not supported.

Why does playback stop and wait?

That is Wait mode. Playback pauses until you play the right notes, so you cannot fall behind. Turn it off in the player if you would rather the music keep going.

The piano part is silent

That is intended — see the first question. The app sounds the accompaniment; the piano is yours to play.

Privacy

Midiful works entirely on your device, makes no network requests, and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
