Connecting a keyboard

USB MIDI keyboards connect on their own. For Bluetooth MIDI, open Settings ▸ Keyboard ▸ Connect Bluetooth MIDI. iOS may ask you to pair again after the app has been closed. Without a keyboard, play the keys on screen.

Adding songs

Tap + to import MIDI (.mid) or MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl) files, or share them to Midiful from Files, Mail or AirDrop. Link a folder to have every file you put in it appear on its own.

Sound

Midiful plays the accompaniment; your keyboard plays your part. If the sound stops after switching apps, return to Midiful — it restarts audio automatically.

Privacy

Midiful works offline and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
