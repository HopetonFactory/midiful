Privacy Policy

Midiful is designed to be private. The app works entirely on your device.

Last updated: 2026-10-15

What we collect

Nothing. Midiful does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your content

MIDI files you import and your settings are stored only on your device (in the app's sandbox). They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Folders you link

If you link a folder, Midiful reads the music files in that folder — and only that folder — to copy them into the app. It never changes or deletes your files.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard you choose to pair.

Network

Midiful makes no network requests of its own. It functions fully offline.

Purchases

There are none. Midiful is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscription, so no payment information is ever involved.

Third parties

The app bundles open-source components (the Bravura music font, the MIDIKit library, and the FluidR3 GM sound font) that run locally and do not collect data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
