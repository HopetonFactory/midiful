Privacy Policy

Midiful · last updated 11 November 2026

Midiful collects nothing. The app works entirely on your device and makes no network requests at all.

What is collected

Nothing. Midiful does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Your content

MIDI files you import, your practice settings and your progress are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Network

The app makes no network requests and works fully offline. It has no server, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

Connected keyboards

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, the notes you play are used on the device to follow along with the music and are discarded as you play. They are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Third-party components

Midiful bundles open-source components that run locally and collect no data: the Bravura music font (SIL OFL 1.1), the MIDIKit library (MIT) and the FluidR3 GM sound font (MIT). None of them makes network requests.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
